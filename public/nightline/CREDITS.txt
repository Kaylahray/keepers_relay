Nightline GT — Asset Credits & Licenses
=======================================

All 3D models, PBR textures, HDRI environments, and audio in this project are
released under CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication) and bundled locally
so the application runs reliably without external CDN dependencies.

3D Models (glTF 2.0 / Binary .glb in /public/models/)
-----------------------------------------------------
- car.glb — Multi-part GT sports coupe chassis, canopy, aero wing, splitter,
  and 5-spoke wheel/brake assembly. Authored procedurally via Three.js geometry
  pipeline (scripts/generate-glbs.ts). License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
- harbour_props.glb — Corrugated shipping container, harbour gantry crane,
  mooring bollard, and overhead neon sign gantry. Authored procedurally.
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
- switchback_props.glb — Multi-tier mountain conifer tree, fractured rock
  outcrop, curved highway lamp post, and steel guardrail post. Authored
  procedurally. License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
- oldtown_props.glb — European stone townhouse facade with pitched roof and
  lit window frames, and wrought-iron street lantern. Authored procedurally.
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
- shared_props.glb — Holographic item pickup cube and concrete/steel road
  barricade. Authored procedurally. License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
